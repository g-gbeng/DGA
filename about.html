Our Story

A Journey That Began in 2012

Digital Global Associates first began as sub-contractors providing trenching and other construction services to a range of civil projects in Nigeria.

Our industry expertise, combined with our excellent service delivery strategy and after-sales support, has positioned us favourably before our clients. Today, we have become one of the fastest-growing indigenous civil construction and engineering works solution providers in the construction industry, with significant footprints across multiple projects.

Digital Global Associates Construction Company Limited will continuously explore opportunities that position us to set irreducible standards of service delivery in Nigeria's construction industry while growing our presence as a valuable growth partner in Africa.

Our People

Leadership & Team

Meet the professionals responsible for providing leadership, technical expertise, and effective project delivery across Digital Global Associates Construction Company Limited.

Chief Executive Officer

Onuchukwu Kenechukwu

CEO / Principal Architect

Kenechukwu Onuchukwu is an architect and the Chief Executive Officer of Digital Global Associates Construction Company Limited, where he provides strategic and organizational leadership and oversees the company's day-to-day operations.

Since the formation of the company, he has brought extensive professional experience to its growth and development, helping to sustain its core values and standards. He has successfully coordinated large multidisciplinary teams comprising quantity surveyors, builders, interior designers, electrical engineers, tilers, and other professionals to deliver multiple turnkey projects across Nigeria.

He also plays a key role in expanding the company's client base and strengthening its presence across West Africa.

Our Team

Experienced Professionals

Okonkwo Dozie

Head of Operations / Civil Engineer

Dozie Okonkwo is a civil engineer with nearly a decade of experience spanning design, building and construction, site supervision, and operations. His extensive site experience enables him to contribute effectively to the technical and operational delivery of projects.

His role involves coordinating effective internal communication and ensuring that external stakeholders, particularly clients and investors, have access to clear and relevant information to support informed decision-making.

Udeaja Obumneme

Senior Site Supervisor

Obumneme Udeaja brings five years of field experience across the civil engineering and construction value chain. A graduate of Nnamdi Azikiwe University, Awka, he joined Digital Global Associates Construction Company Limited in 2018.

Nwadiogbu Chuba

Head, Design, Safety & Sustainability / Architect

Chuba Nwadiogbu is an architect responsible for developing plans and designs that support Digital Global Associates Construction Company Limited in delivering practical and effective solutions for its clients.

His responsibilities include feasibility studies, environmental impact considerations, site selection, cost analysis, land-use studies, and defining project design requirements. With experience in architectural design, visualization, and project management, he is involved throughout the project lifecycle, from initial client discussions to final delivery.

Nwachukwu Augustine

Head, Training & Development / Building Technologist

Augustine Nwachukwu is a professional building technologist with a strong commitment to due process, technical excellence, and quality project delivery.

He oversees technical processes and construction methods, combining emerging technologies with local expertise to translate project designs from concept to reality. His responsibilities include ensuring appropriate material selection, compliance with applicable building regulations, technical staff development, and effective coordination of project resources.

Orizu Ifenna

Team Lead, Testing & Quality Control / Assurance

Ifenna Orizu is a graduate of Agricultural and Bio-Resources Engineering from Michael Okpara University of Agriculture, Umudike. He contributes to project quality and technical decision-making through his expertise in soil testing and analysis.

His responsibilities include assessing soil characteristics such as bearing capacity, density, compaction, sand content, and potential contaminants before construction begins. He works closely with structural engineers by providing essential soil information required for the effective design of foundations, footings, slabs, and other structural elements.

Our Commitment

Health, Safety & Environmental Policy

At Digital Global Associates Construction Company Limited, we recognize that our people are the foundation of our success. We are committed to protecting their health, ensuring their safety, and providing a safe working environment that supports their well-being, productivity, and professional development.

We recognize that compliance with applicable legislation, regulations, and recognized health, safety, and environmental practices is essential to responsible business operations. We strive to identify, assess, and manage potential and existing risks associated with our activities while protecting the environment and continuously improving the quality and safety of our projects.

Our policy applies across our operations and provides a framework for consistent health, safety, and environmental practices, while allowing individual operations to adopt procedures that align with its principles and objectives.

Employee Health & Safety

Protect the health, safety, and welfare of employees and others who may be directly affected by our operations.

Environmental Protection

Conduct our activities responsibly, comply with applicable environmental requirements, and minimize the environmental impact of our operations through sustainable and cost-effective practices.

Training & Awareness

Provide regular employee training and promote strict adherence to health, safety, and environmental policies and procedures.

Monitoring & Continuous Improvement

Establish clear responsibilities, reporting structures, and monitoring systems to review compliance and continually improve safety and quality control measures.

Risk Prevention & Stop-Work Commitment

Encourage employees to stop any activity immediately when they identify a breach of our HSE policy or a condition that compromises health, safety, or environmental protection.

Emergency Preparedness

Develop and maintain appropriate emergency response systems to address accidents, hazards, and other emergencies arising from our operations.

Shared Responsibility

Promote shared responsibility between management, employees, and business partners, ensuring that partner HSE practices substantially align with our policy requirements.
