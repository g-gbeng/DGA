Our Story

A Journey That Began in 2012

Digital Global Associates first began as sub-contractors providing trenching and other construction services to a range of civil projects in Nigeria.

Our industry expertise, combined with our excellent service delivery strategy and after-sales support, has positioned us favourably before our clients. Today, we have become one of the fastest-growing indigenous civil construction and engineering works solution providers in the construction industry, with significant footprints across multiple projects.

Over time, Digital Global Associates Construction Company Limited has served and continues to serve the civil construction and engineering needs of private and corporate clients across various sectors of the Nigerian economy.

Digital Global Associates Construction Company Limited will continuously explore opportunities that position us to set irreducible standards of service delivery in Nigeria's construction industry while growing our presence as a valuable growth partner in Africa.

Our Commitment

Health, Safety & Environmental Policy

At Digital Global Associates Construction Company Limited, we recognize that our people are the foundation of our success. We are committed to protecting their health, ensuring their safety, and providing a safe working environment that supports their well-being, productivity, and professional development.

We recognize that compliance with applicable legislation, regulations, and recognized health, safety, and environmental practices is essential to responsible business operations. We strive to identify, assess, and manage potential and existing risks associated with our activities while protecting the environment and continuously improving the quality and safety of our projects.

Our policy applies across our operations and provides a framework for consistent health, safety, and environmental practices, while allowing individual operations to adopt procedures that align with its principles and objectives.

Employee Health & Safety

Protect the health, safety, and welfare of employees and others who may be directly affected by our operations.

Environmental Protection

Conduct our activities responsibly, comply with applicable environmental requirements, and minimize the environmental impact of our operations through sustainable and cost-effective practices.

Training & Awareness

Provide regular employee training and promote strict adherence to health, safety, and environmental policies and procedures.

Monitoring & Continuous Improvement

Establish clear responsibilities, reporting structures, and monitoring systems to review compliance and continually improve safety and quality control measures.

Risk Prevention & Stop-Work Commitment

Encourage employees to stop any activity immediately when they identify a breach of our HSE policy or a condition that compromises health, safety, or environmental protection.

Emergency Preparedness

Develop and maintain appropriate emergency response systems to address accidents, hazards, and other emergencies arising from our operations.

Shared Responsibility

Promote shared responsibility between management, employees, and business partners, ensuring that partner HSE practices substantially align with our policy requirements.
