Our Story

A Journey That Began in 2012

Digital Global Associates first began as sub-contractors providing trenching and other construction services to a range of civil projects in Nigeria.

Our industry expertise, combined to our excellent service delivery strategy and after sales support has put us in the right stead before our clients and today, we have become one of the fastest growing, indigenous civil construction and engineering works solution providers in the construction industry with significant footprints across multpile projects.

Overtime, Digital Global Associates Construction Company Limited has served and is still serving the civil construction and engineering works needs of a cross section of private and corporate clients cutting across various sectors of the Nigerian economy.

Digital Global Associates Construction Company Limited will continuously explore opportunities that will position her to set irreducible standards of service delivery in Nigeria’s construction industry while growing its presence as a valuable growth partners in Africa.
